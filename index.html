About

Welcome to Genna's Underground Confections

I’m Genna, and for about the past year or so, I’ve been hyper-fixated on baking. Friends and family keep telling me to open a bakery, but honestly? I cannot be bothered with running a whole business. What I do love is making custom treats for the people I care about — so here we are.

Why “Underground”?

Because I’m baking out of my home kitchen (with my pets, Nova and Demi), and Pennsylvania, of course, has a lot of laws and regulations about home bakeries. So, this isn’t a business — it’s a tasty, little side-quest.

Why “Confections”?

Because I stick to sweets: cupcakes, cookies, cheesecakes, and fun flavor experiments.
Bread? Cinnamon rolls?Anything dough-related? Absolutely not.

I’m self-taught, and I’ll be the first to admit my bakes aren’t the prettiest — but where the beauty lacks, the flavor punches you in the face (figuratively, of course). I don’t believe you should have to “search” for flavor in anything: wine, beer, ice cream, cakes, whatever. If it’s supposed to taste like something, it should taste like something.

Everything I make is from scratch, so no boxed mixes or store-bought frosting here. Sometimes I’ll use store-bought jams or curds, but the core of every bake is all me.

I love unique flavor combos, so let’s get creative and go a little wild. Send me a request — I can’t wait to wow you with my sweets!
